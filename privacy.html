Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Apatmento ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number
  • Booking information: dates, guest counts, payment references
  • Host information: property details, pricing, M-Pesa payout number
  • Communications: reviews, messages, support requests

We also collect information automatically:

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, search queries
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, IP address
  • Location data: only when you use the Emergency SOS feature and grant permission

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process bookings and payments
  • To connect guests with hosts
  • To send booking confirmations and receipts
  • To improve the platform and resolve disputes
  • To send service updates (you can opt out at any time)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Payment Data

Payments are processed via M-Pesa through PayHero. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or full payment credentials. We store only transaction references and amounts necessary to confirm bookings. Your M-Pesa number is stored to enable payouts to hosts.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Hosts/Operators: your name and contact details for confirmed bookings
  • Payment processors: PayHero (for M-Pesa transactions)
  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database), Netlify (hosting) — under data processing agreements
  • Law enforcement: when required by Kenyan law

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Booking records are retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes. You can request deletion of your account at any time — see Section 7.

6. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for authentication (Supabase session tokens). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. You can clear cookies via your browser settings, which will log you out of your account.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data via your account settings
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@apatmento.com

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure database storage via Supabase, and server-side environment variables for all sensitive credentials. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext.

9. Children's Privacy

Apatmento is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
