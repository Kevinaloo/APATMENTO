Questions, answered

Everything about Cabana.

Is Cabana the same as Apatmento?
Yes. Cabana is the new name for Apatmento. The team, the platform, and the zero-commission promise are exactly the same, only the name is changing. Your account, bookings and listings all carry over.
What is Cabana?
Cabana is a zero-commission travel super-app, born in Nairobi and built for Africa and beyond. Book short-stay apartments, flights, tours, events, rides, food and more from one account. Hosts keep 100% of what they earn and guests pay only face value, no hidden fees.
What can I book on Cabana?

How does zero commission work?
The price a host sets is the price a host keeps. Cabana takes 0% of it. Guests pay only a small fixed booking fee, never a percentage, so what you see is what you pay. No service-fee surprises at checkout, for either side.
Do I need a new account for Cabana?
No. Everything stays where it is. Sign in exactly as you always have. The same login works, and all your bookings and saved places are intact.
