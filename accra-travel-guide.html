Accra Travel Guide 2026

Ghana's capital is West Africa's most welcoming major city. Vibrant arts scene, extraordinary jollof, Labadi Beach, and the legacy of the Year of Return that made Accra the global home of the African diaspora.

Accra at a glance

Accra is the political and commercial capital of Ghana and one of West Africa's most dynamic cities. At approximately 5 million people, it is smaller and more manageable than Lagos but equally vibrant in culture, food and economic activity. Ghana's political stability (unbroken democratic governance since 1992), warm hospitality and openness to the African diaspora have made Accra a rising star on the global travel map.

Population: approximately 5 million. Currency: Ghana Cedi (GHS). Language: English (official), Twi, Ga. Time zone: GMT (same as London in winter, 1 hour behind in summer. Ghana does not observe daylight saving).

Best areas to stay in Accra

  • East Legon: Accra's premier residential suburb. American Embassy, best restaurants, reliable electricity. GHS 350–900/night.
  • Airport Residential Area (ARA): business hub, all embassies, Accra Mall adjacent. GHS 350–900/night.
  • Cantonments: diplomatic quarter, quiet streets, embassy residences. GHS 400–1,000/night.
  • Osu: entertainment spine. Oxford Street, restaurants, nightlife. GHS 300–700/night.
  • Labone: residential, excellent restaurants, quieter than Osu. GHS 350–800/night.
  • Adabraka / North Ridge: central, budget-friendly, local character. GHS 200–450/night.

Top things to do in Accra

Culture & Heritage

  • Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum: the memorial and resting place of Ghana's founding father. Beautiful park and museum in central Accra. Free–GHS 30.
  • National Museum of Ghana: collections spanning Ghanaian history from prehistoric times to the present. Barnes Road, Adabraka.
  • Jamestown & Ussher Town: Accra's historic colonial quarters. The James Fort, Ussher Fort, the fishing harbour, and extraordinary street photography. Walking tour recommended.
  • WEB Du Bois Centre: the home and resting place of the African-American intellectual giant who chose Ghana as his final home. Must-visit for diaspora visitors.
  • Accra Art Market: Nkrumah Circle. Authentic crafts, kente, adinkra, beads and art. Negotiate confidently.

Beaches

  • Labadi Beach: Accra's most popular beach. Reggae, food vendors, beach bars. Weekends are vibrant and crowded. GHS 20 entry.
  • Kokrobite: 30 km west of Accra. Quieter, surfable Atlantic waves. Big Milly's Backyard is a Ghanaian institution. Day trip or overnight.
  • Bojo Beach: island accessible by canoe from the mainland. Peaceful, pristine. GHS 30–40 including canoe.

Arts & Nightlife

  • Alliance Française d'Accra: excellent programme of art exhibitions, film, music and theatre. Off Liberation Road.
  • Artist Alliance Gallery: the finest contemporary Ghanaian art. Osu.
  • Firefly Bar: rooftop bar with Accra's best city views. Airport Residential Area.
  • Skybar 25: rooftop pool bar overlooking Accra. Popular for sundowners.

Food in Accra

Ghanaian food is among the most distinctive in Africa, and Accra has the continent's most underrated dining scene.

  • Jollof rice: the great West African jollof debate (Ghana vs Nigeria) begins here. Ghanaian jollof is smoky and tomato-rich. Try at Azmera or local chop bars.
  • Kelewele: spiced, fried plantain. The definitive Ghanaian street food. Osu's Oxford Street at night.
  • Fufu with light soup: pounded cassava and plantain, served with palm nut or groundnut soup and goat/chicken. A labour of love and a Ghanaian soul food.
  • Waakye: rice and beans cooked in sorghum husks (gives it the distinctive reddish-brown colour), served with fried fish, plantain, spaghetti and a boiled egg. The definitive Accra breakfast/lunch.
  • Chichinga: skewered, spiced grilled meat. Best from street vendors in Accra's evening markets.

Best restaurants in Accra

  • Buka: finest West African restaurant in Accra. East Legon. Reservations essential weekends.
  • Santoku: exceptional Japanese-Ghanaian fusion. East Legon.
  • COCO Lounge: Accra institution. Airport Residential. Multi-cuisine, reliable quality.
  • The Kitchen by Wazobia: Nigerian-Ghanaian fusion. Cantonments.
  • Nanzeen: Lebanese. East Legon. Reliable and extensive.

Day trips from Accra

  • Cape Coast & Elmina: 145 km, 2.5–3 hours. Elmina Castle, Cape Coast Castle, Kakum canopy walk. Most important day trip from Accra. An essential experience for every visitor to Ghana.
  • Akosombo Dam & Lake Volta: 100 km north-east. The world's largest man-made lake by surface area. The dam itself is an engineering marvel. Day trip or overnight on Lake Volta.
  • Kokrobite Beach: 30 km west. Surf beach, Big Milly's Backyard, relaxed Atlantic coast. Easy half-day from Accra.
  • Shai Hills Resource Reserve: 60 km east. Baboons, rock hyrax, birds, cave system. Good hiking. Half-day.

Practical information

  • Visa: Ghana offers visa on arrival for most nationalities (USD 50–150 depending on nationality). E-visa available at evisa.gov.gh.
  • Currency: Ghana Cedi (GHS). ATMs widely available. Forex bureaux give better rates. MoMo (MTN Mobile Money) is widely accepted.
  • Getting around: Uber and Bolt operate throughout Accra. Trotros (shared minibuses) are local but complex for first-time visitors. Taxi hire by the day (GHS 200–400) for touring is efficient.
  • Kotoka Airport: In the heart of Accra, 15–25 minutes from most central neighbourhoods. One of West Africa's best-connected airports. Direct flights from London Heathrow, Washington Dulles, Amsterdam, Dubai, and across Africa.
  • Best time to visit: November–March and July–August (dry periods). April–June is the main rainy season; September–October has lighter rains.

Frequently asked questions

Is Accra safe for tourists?
Yes. Accra is consistently rated West Africa's safest capital for visitors. Standard urban precautions apply. East Legon, Airport Residential, Cantonments and Osu are all very safe for tourists.
What is the best area to stay in Accra?
East Legon for the best restaurants and infrastructure. Airport Residential for business and embassy proximity. Osu for nightlife and entertainment. Cantonments for diplomatic quarter calm.
How far is Cape Coast from Accra?
About 145 km, 2.5–3 hours by road. VIP and STC buses depart regularly from Accra. The drive takes you through Ghana's coastal landscape. Most visitors do Cape Coast as a 2-day trip, staying overnight to visit both Elmina and Cape Coast Castles.
What is waakye in Accra?
Waakye (pronounced 'watch-ee') is Ghana's most beloved street food. Rice and beans cooked with dried sorghum husks that give it a distinctive reddish-brown colour and earthy flavour. Served with fried fish or chicken, sweet plantain, boiled egg, avocado, and spaghetti. The complete Ghanaian culinary experience in a single dish.
