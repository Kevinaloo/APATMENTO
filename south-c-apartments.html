South C: affordable central Nairobi

South C and South B together form one of Nairobi's most established middle-class residential zones. The area has excellent infrastructure. Naivas and Carrefour supermarkets, T-Mall and the South C shopping centre, reliable water supply and regular Uber availability.

Wilson Airport (for domestic and East African flights on Safarilink, Airkenya and others) is 10 minutes away. JKIA is 20–25 minutes. The CBD is 15 minutes in normal traffic. Karen and Langata are 15–20 minutes south.

For guests on business or looking to avoid Nairobi's premium suburb pricing while remaining fully connected, South C offers excellent value.

What's nearby

South C has direct access to Langata Road, the Southern Bypass, T-Mall, the Carnivore restaurant, Bomas of Kenya (cultural centre), and Nairobi National Park entrance.

South C: frequently asked

Is South C safe in Nairobi?
South C is one of Nairobi's more established residential areas with an active community. Standard Nairobi urban precautions apply. The suburb is generally calm and suitable for short stays.
How far is South C from JKIA?
About 15–20 minutes by car in clear traffic. Via the Southern Bypass, JKIA is a straightforward drive.
What is near South C Nairobi?
Wilson Airport (domestic), Carnivore restaurant, Bomas of Kenya, T-Mall, Nairobi National Park entrance, and Lang'ata Link Road.
