Global: frequently asked

Is Cabana available worldwide?
Yes. Cabana operates across Africa, Europe, Asia, the Americas and Oceania. Any host anywhere in the world can list at zero commission. Any guest can book from anywhere.
How does Cabana compare to Airbnb globally?
Cabana charges zero commission globally. Airbnb charges hosts up to 20% and guests 6–12%. Cabana also offers Cabana Match (reverse marketplace), APA AI concierge, and M-Pesa payments, all unique to Cabana worldwide.
How do I list my property on Cabana from outside Africa?
Go to cabana.africa/become-partner. Create a free host account, list your property and set your own price. Zero commission, zero listing fee, go live in under 10 minutes. Open to hosts in every country.
What is Cabana Match?
Cabana Match is the world's first reverse travel marketplace. Instead of searching for a listing, guests post their travel requirement (dates, location, budget, preferences) and verified Cabana hosts come to them with tailored offers. The guest chooses the best. No other platform in the world offers this.
