Frequently asked questions

How do I buy event tickets?

Tap any event to see the details, pick a ticket tier and pay by M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or card. You get a code to show at the door, at the organiser’s own price with no booking levy from us.

What is Cabana Live Premium?

Premium opens every live show, concert stream, movie and series on Cabana Live. Your first month is free: sign in, start your free month, and everything opens straight away. No card is needed.

Where does the music chart come from?

The Cabana Top 50 is compiled from YouTube music activity for Kenya and refreshed through the day. Press play on any record and it keeps playing while you browse the rest of Cabana Live.

Are the ticket prices the final price?

Yes. The price shown is the organiser's own price. We add nothing on top.
