Seoul's best neighbourhoods

Gangnam: Korea's most famous neighbourhood after PSY made it global. COEX Mall, luxury brands, K-beauty flagships, the best department stores.
Hongdae: university neighbourhood. Street performances, indie music, the most vibrant young energy in Seoul, great nightlife.
Itaewon: international neighbourhood. Every type of cuisine, LGBTQ+ friendly, Haebangchon (HBC) adjacent.
Insadong: traditional Korean culture. Teahouses, galleries, celadon ceramics, hanbok rental.
Bukchon Hanok Village: preserved traditional Korean houses (hanok). The most photographed neighbourhood in Seoul.
Myeongdong: K-beauty shopping district. Every Korean skincare brand's flagship store.

Getting around Seoul

Seoul's subway is one of the world's finest, 9 lines, incredibly efficient, English signage throughout. T-money card for all transport including airport. Incheon Airport (ICN) is 60–70 minutes by AREX express train.

Seoul: frequently asked

What is Seoul famous for?
K-pop (Big Hit Entertainment, SM Entertainment, YG all based here), Korean BBQ (samgyeopsal, galbi, bulgogi), jimjilbang (24-hour bathhouses), the Han River, Changdeokgung Palace (UNESCO), Bukchon Hanok Village, and one of the world's most advanced tech cultures.
Is Seoul safe?
Seoul is one of the world's safest large cities. Crime rates are extremely low. Women travelling alone consistently rate Seoul as one of the most comfortable cities globally.
What is Korean BBQ?
Korean BBQ (KBBQ) is a dining experience where meat. Samgyeopsal (pork belly), galbi (beef short rib), bulgogi (marinated beef). Is grilled at the table over charcoal or gas. Accompanied by banchan (small side dishes) and wrapped in lettuce. One of the world's great dining cultures.
