What we need

What it takes to be approved

We check every driver properly, because a rider who trusts the fleet books again. Nothing here is unusual. If you already drive commercially in Nairobi you almost certainly have it.

You

  • Kenyan national ID or valid passport
  • Driving licence held for at least three years
  • PSV badge, current
  • Certificate of good conduct issued within the last twelve months
  • A smartphone that can run the driver console and share location

Your vehicle

  • Logbook in your name, or a signed owner authority
  • Comprehensive insurance with PSV cover, current
  • Valid NTSA inspection certificate
  • Working seatbelts on every seat and a clean interior
  • 2014 or newer for Economy, 2018 or newer for Comfort and above

The standard

  • Accept or decline quickly, so riders are not left waiting
  • Arrive at the pick-up point, not the general area
  • Help with luggage on airport and transfer work
  • Keep the rating above 4.6 across your last fifty trips
  • Take the induction before your first trip. It takes an hour
Classes

Your car decides what work you get offered

Every driver starts on Economy. Once your documents and vehicle are verified we clear you for the classes your car qualifies for, and higher classes pay considerably more per kilometre. You cannot promote your own car. An inspection decides it.

Driver questions

What does Cabana actually take?

Ten percent of the fare. Nothing else. There is no joining fee, no weekly subscription, no charge for the app, and no deduction for support or dispatch. On a KSh 1,000 trip you receive KSh 900. The rider sees that split on their own fare breakdown before they book, which is deliberate: it keeps us honest with both of you.

When exactly am I paid?

Every Monday morning, covering the week from Monday to Sunday just ended. Payment goes to the M-Pesa number on your account. There is no minimum balance to reach and no withdrawal fee from us. Cash fares stay with you and are reconciled against your account on the same weekly cycle.

Do I have to hit a target or stay online for set hours?

No. There is no daily target, no minimum shift and no penalty for being offline. What does matter is what you do when you are online: if you take a trip, take it properly, and if you cannot, decline it quickly so the rider goes to somebody who can.

Can I drive a car that is not in my name?

Yes, with a signed authority from the registered owner and a copy of their ID alongside the logbook. The insurance must cover you by name as a driver. If you drive for a fleet owner, the owner can register the vehicles and add you as a driver on them.

What is airport and chauffeur work, and how do I get it?

Fixed-price airport transfers and full-day chauffeur bookings pay considerably more than a metered city trip, and they are assigned rather than broadcast. Drivers become eligible after fifty completed trips with a rating of 4.8 or better, a Comfort-class vehicle or above, and the extended induction covering arrivals protocol and luggage handling.

What happens if a document expires?

We record the expiry date on every document when you join and warn you three weeks, one week and one day before. On the expiry date the account pauses automatically and you stop receiving trips. Send the renewed document and you are back online the same day. Nothing is deleted and you do not reapply.

Is my location tracked when I am offline?

No. Your phone reports position only while you are online in the driver console. Go offline and the reporting stops. While you are online, riders never see your position until you have accepted their trip, and after that they see only your car on the way to them.
