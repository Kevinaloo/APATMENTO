Medellín: the phoenix city

Medellín sits in a narrow mountain valley in the Colombian Andes at 1,495m elevation. The 'City of Eternal Spring' for its year-round 24°C average temperature. The metamorphosis from cartel capital to innovation hub is one of history's most remarkable urban transformations.

El Poblado: Medellín's international neighbourhood. Parque El Poblado, the Zona Rosa, every type of restaurant, the best nightlife. Where most short-stay guests base themselves.
Laureles: residential, leafy, excellent local restaurants, calmer than El Poblado. The authentic Medellín neighbourhood experience.
Envigado: Pablo Escobar's former home municipality, now one of Medellín's safest and most pleasant suburbs.
El Centro: the beating commercial heart. Chaotic, authentic, the Plaza Botero (Botero sculptures outdoors, free to visit).

The Metro is one of Latin America's most efficient. Connecting the valley floor with cable cars (Metrocable) ascending to hilltop comunas like Santo Domingo.

Why Medellín for digital nomads

Year-round perfect climate, fast internet, world-class co-working (Selina, Selina, dozens of independent spaces), cost of living 70% lower than Western Europe or North America, extraordinary food, thriving expat community and safety transformation make Medellín the #1 digital nomad destination in Latin America by most rankings.

Medellín: frequently asked

Is Medellín safe now?
Yes. Medellín is now significantly safer than many major Western cities. El Poblado, Laureles and Envigado are very safe for tourists. Standard urban precautions apply; some areas (El Centro at night) require more care.
What is Medellín famous for today?
Innovation (Ruta N technology district), design (Medellín Design Centre), the incredible cable car sistema connecting hillside comunas, the world-class Museo de Antioquia (Botero collection), and the most vibrant nightlife in South America outside Buenos Aires.
What is the weather like in Medellín?
Medellín earns 'City of Eternal Spring' because altitude (1,495m) keeps it at a constant 22–27°C year-round. No extreme summer heat, no cold winters. Light rains in April–May and October–November; January–March and July–August are driest.
