Santorini: where to stay

Oia: the famous blue domes. Most photographed village in Greece. Premium prices for caldera-edge suites. Book well in advance.
Imerovigli: 'balcony of Santorini'. The highest point of the caldera. Quieter than Oia, equally spectacular views, better value.
Fira: the capital. Most central, best nightlife, caldera views, shopping. Busiest area.
Firostefani: between Fira and Imerovigli. Caldera edge at slightly lower prices than Oia.
Perissa / Perivolos: the black sand beach villages. Quieter, budget-friendly, very different energy from the caldera villages.

Getting around Santorini

ATV/quad bike or car hire for the island. ATVs cost €25–40/day and are the classic Santorini experience. Local buses connect main villages. Santorini Airport (JTR) has flights from Athens (45 min) and direct summer connections from London, Paris and across Europe.

Santorini: frequently asked

When is the best time to visit Santorini?
May–June and September–October for the best balance of weather, fewer crowds and lower prices. July–August is peak season. Hot, crowded and expensive. April and November are peaceful but some hotels close.
What is the best village in Santorini?
Oia for iconic photographs; Imerovigli for quieter caldera luxury; Fira for nightlife and central access; Perissa for the black sand beach experience.
Why is Santorini so famous?
Santorini is a volcanic caldera. A collapsed supervolcano filled with the Aegean Sea. The cliff-edge villages of whitewashed houses with blue domes create one of the world's most distinctive landscapes.
