Why Kigali

Kigali is extraordinary. Plastic bags are banned, community cleaning (Umuganda) happens every last Saturday of the month, and it is safe in a way few African capitals are. The city has sophisticated dining, excellent coffee culture, and is a growing regional tech and conference hub (CHOGM, Africa CDC, dozens of international summits).

Kimihurura — expat and diplomatic quarter.
Nyarutarama — residential premium. Sports facilities, golf course, top restaurants.
Gacuriro — newer, more affordable, rapidly developing.

Kigali as a gorilla base

Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park is 3 hours from Kigali by road. Gorilla trekking permits cost $1,500 per person but are among Africa's most extraordinary wildlife experiences. Most travellers use Kigali for 2 nights before and after their gorilla trek.

Kigali: frequently asked

Is Kigali the safest city in Africa?
Kigali consistently tops safety rankings for African capitals. EIU Liveability and various safety indices place it among Africa's most secure urban environments. Crime against visitors is very rare.
What is Kigali known for?
Immaculate cleanliness, extraordinary safety, and as the gateway to mountain gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park. Also Africa's premier conference and events destination.
How far are the mountain gorillas from Kigali?
Volcanoes National Park is about 3 hours from Kigali by road. Most operators offer 1- or 2-day gorilla trek packages with Kigali accommodation included.
