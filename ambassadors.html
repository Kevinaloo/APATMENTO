04 / The small print, human sized

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Clear from the first hello.

How do I join?

The programme is invitation-only. Have a connection to make? Talk to the Cabana team. An enquiry doesn't automatically grant access.

What earns commission?

Eligible attributed bookings earn a share of Cabana's service fee, where a fee applies. Commission is not based on the full booking price. The figures are set out in your dashboard after you are invited.

45 days or 365 days?

Two different clocks. A new introduction is reserved for 45 days, first come, first served. The referral period for eligible booking commission lasts 365 days.

Who owns the listing?

The owner does. A listing you prepare stays unpublished until they sign in and accept it. Your dashboard records the handover; registration and owner acceptance are separate milestones.

When can I withdraw?

Your rewards page separates potential earnings awaiting check-in, confirmed earnings on hold and funds ready to withdraw. It also shows your available withdrawal options and requirements.
