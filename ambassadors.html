What the role pays

Paid work, not a lanyard.

Ambassadors are paid for what they build, and paid for a full year after they build it. The figures are set out in your dashboard the day you are invited — they are not published here, because what you earn is between you and Cabana.

Commission
A share of our service fee, for 365 days

On every booking made by every traveller, host and operator you bring — for a full year from the day they join, with no cap on how many you bring. The ambassador rate is higher than the public one.

And beyond commission
Incentives, allowances, and a way in

Milestone incentives for hitting onboarding targets. Airtime, transport and data allowances for field work. Equipment where the work needs it. And first consideration for salaried roles — this team is where we hire from.

One thing said plainly, since the numbers are not here. Cabana's service fee is a small fixed amount per booking, not a percentage of it. Commission is a share of that fee, so it does not grow with the price of a stay — a big villa pays what a small room pays. This programme rewards how many people you bring, not how expensive their holidays are, and everything above is built around that.
How it works

Four things, in order.

1
Claim the lead first
Before you go and talk to someone, claim them in your dashboard. The claim is what reserves them as yours — first come, first served across the whole team. It lapses after 45 days if nothing comes of it, so nobody can sit on a phone book.
2
Do the onboarding with them
Sit with the host and fill the listing in on their phone or yours. You can draft the whole thing for them; it stays a draft until they have an account and accept it. The listing is always theirs, never yours.
3
They go live and start earning
Nothing pays on a signup. It pays when the person you brought actually earns, which is why a listing nobody would book is worth nothing to anyone — including whoever created it.
4
You get paid for a year
Commission lands on every booking they take or make, for 365 days. It is released once each booking is past its cancellation window, then withdrawn to M-Pesa from KES 50.
