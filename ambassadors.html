03 / THE DETAILS, SIMPLIFIED

Good work deserves
clear words.

Two different actions used to share the word “claim”. Here is what each step actually means.

01

Reserve an introduction

You record a person you plan to help. The reservation lasts 45 days and is first come, first served across the team. This does not create an account or transfer a listing.

02

Prepare their listing

You can build a listing on the owner's behalf. The listing stays unpublished while it is waiting for the owner to sign in and accept it.

03

The owner accepts

The owner takes control of the listing in their own account. Your contribution remains visible as an accepted listing, even though it is now theirs to manage.

04

Eligible bookings earn commission

Commission is based on Cabana's service fee for eligible attributed bookings during the 365-day referral period. Registration and owner acceptance are recorded milestones; earnings begin with qualifying booking activity.

04 / KNOW WHERE YOU STAND

A clear role.
A considered welcome.

Who can become an ambassador?

The programme is invitation-only. You can explore this page without an account. If the work sounds like you, contact the Cabana team to discuss joining. An enquiry does not automatically grant access.

What is the commission based on?

A share of Cabana's service fee, where a fee applies, rather than the full booking price. The figures are set out in your dashboard after you are invited. The referral period lasts 365 days; the reservation for a new introduction lasts 45 days. These are different clocks.

When does money become available?

Qualifying bookings create commission records. Potential earnings awaiting check-in, confirmed earnings on hold and money ready to withdraw are shown separately. Your rewards page shows the available withdrawal options and requirements.

What happens after the owner accepts?

The listing belongs to the owner. Your dashboard keeps a record of the handover and recognises the accepted listing. A person can accept more than one listing, so “owners who accepted” and “accepted listings” can have different totals.

Is a registration the same as an accepted listing?

No. A registration means someone created an account. An accepted listing means an owner explicitly took over a listing you prepared. A reserved introduction is earlier still. Your dashboard shows each stage separately so you can understand what has actually happened.
