Frequently asked questions

How do I order food delivery?

Browse the restaurants on this page, tap the one you want, and you'll be taken to their live menu. Add items to your cart, enter your delivery address, and pay. Your food is prepared fresh and delivered to your door with live tracking.

Which restaurants deliver near me?

Top-rated local restaurants across a wide range of cuisines offer delivery through our platform. Use the filters and search above to find what's available in your area.

How much does food delivery cost?

Delivery fees vary by restaurant and distance, and are shown clearly at checkout. Many restaurants also run offers and promotions. Prices are quoted in Kenyan Shillings (KES).

What areas do you deliver to?

Delivery is available across a growing number of towns and neighbourhoods, depending on each restaurant's delivery radius. We're continually expanding coverage.
