Restaurants, menus and prices across Kenya

Every restaurant on this page put itself here. The name, the photographs, the dishes and the price against each dish were typed in by the kitchen that cooks them. Cabana does not mark anything up, does not take a cut of the bill and does not sit between you and the counter. What you read is what you pay.

That also means the page is only as full as the kitchens on it. There are no sample restaurants padding out the grid and no borrowed catalogue from somewhere else. A quiet week looks quiet. When a kitchen adds a dish at four in the afternoon, it is on this page at four in the afternoon.

What you will find

Nyama choma over charcoal, Swahili pilau and coconut fish, biryani on a Friday, tandoor and curry, wood-fired pizza, burgers, shawarma at midnight, mandazi and chai before six in the morning. Kitchens mark their own dishes as new, as the plate their regulars always order, or as tonight's offer, and those move to the front of this page on their own.

Ordering

Open a restaurant, read the menu, then send the order straight to the kitchen on WhatsApp or ring the number on the page. The kitchen confirms what it costs and how long it takes. Most take M-Pesa, many take cash or card at the counter. Delivery, collection and eating in are marked separately so you know which ones a kitchen actually does.

Running a kitchen

Putting a restaurant up takes about four minutes and costs nothing. After that the menu lives in your partner hub: add a dish, set a price, photograph the plate, mark it as a favourite, put an offer on it for the weekend and take the offer off on Monday. You keep every shilling a diner pays you.

Questions people ask

How do I order from a restaurant on Cabana?

Open a restaurant, read its menu, then send your order to the kitchen on WhatsApp or call the number on the page. The kitchen confirms the price and the time, and you pay them for the food.

Are the menu prices real?

Yes. Every dish and price on a restaurant page was typed in by that restaurant. Cabana adds no markup and takes no commission from what the kitchen charges.

Which restaurants are listed?

Only restaurants that have signed up and published their own menu. There are no sample or placeholder restaurants here, so if a kitchen appears it is a real kitchen you can order from.

How does a restaurant get listed?

Publish the restaurant from the Cabana partner hub, then add dishes with prices and photos from your phone. Mark a dish as new, as a favourite or as an offer and it moves to the front of this page. It is free and there is no commission.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. Restaurants on Cabana take M-Pesa, and many take cash or card at the counter as well.
