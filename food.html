Restaurants, menus and prices, written by the kitchens themselves.

Every restaurant on this page put itself here. The name, the photographs, the dishes and the price against each dish were typed in by the kitchen that cooks them. Cabana does not mark anything up, does not take a cut of the bill and does not sit between you and the counter. What you read is what you pay.

That also means the page is only as full as the kitchens on it. There are no sample restaurants padding out the grid and no borrowed catalogue from somewhere else. A quiet week looks quiet. When a kitchen adds a dish at four in the afternoon, it is on this page at four in the afternoon.

What you will find

Nyama choma over charcoal, Swahili pilau and coconut fish, biryani on a Friday, tandoor and curry, wood-fired pizza, burgers, shawarma at midnight, mandazi and chai before six in the morning. Kitchens mark their own dishes as new, as the plate their regulars always order, or as tonight's offer, and those move to the front of this page on their own.

What the time of day changes

This page knows what time it is. In the morning it puts breakfast first, at midday lunch, in the evening whatever is coming off the grill. That is all it does: it changes the order things appear in, never what is on the page. Nyama choma is still here at seven in the morning and mandazi are still here at midnight, a little further down, and one tap on another service brings them straight back to the top. Nothing is ever withheld until a permitted hour.

Ordering

Tap a dish to open the kitchen that cooks it and read the whole menu. Add what you want to your order as you go. When you are done, the checkout collects your name, your number and where to bring it, then hands each kitchen a complete ticket you send them on WhatsApp or read out by phone. The kitchen confirms what it costs and how long it takes. Most take M-Pesa, many take cash or card at the counter. Delivery, collection and eating in are marked separately so you know which ones a kitchen actually does.

Ordering from more than one kitchen

Your order can hold food from as many kitchens as you like. Nobody should have to choose between the biryani from one counter and the cake from another, so the checkout keeps them apart: each kitchen gets its own ticket, its own total and its own delivery arrangement, because each one is a separate kitchen cooking separate food. Your order is kept on your device for twelve hours, so you can leave, look elsewhere and come back to it.

Running a kitchen

Putting a restaurant up takes about four minutes and costs nothing. After that the menu lives in your partner hub: add a dish, set a price, photograph the plate, mark it as a favourite, put an offer on it for the weekend and take the offer off on Monday. You keep every shilling a diner pays you.

Questions people ask

How do I order food on Cabana?

Tap any dish to open the kitchen's menu, add what you want to your order, then go to the checkout. The checkout writes each kitchen a complete ticket with your name, number and address on it, which you send them on WhatsApp or read out by phone. The kitchen confirms the price and the time, and you pay them directly for the food.

Can I order from more than one restaurant at once?

Yes. Your order can hold food from as many kitchens as you like. At the checkout each kitchen gets its own ticket with its own total, because each one cooks, prices and delivers its own food. You send them one at a time and pay each kitchen for what they cooked.

Does the page hide food that does not suit the time of day?

No. The page puts breakfast first in the morning and dinner first in the evening, but that only changes the order dishes appear in. Everything the kitchens are cooking stays on the page at every hour, and tapping another meal at the top brings it straight to the front.

Are the menu prices real?

Yes. Every dish and price on a restaurant page was typed in by that restaurant. Cabana adds no markup and takes no commission from what the kitchen charges.

Which restaurants are listed?

Only restaurants that have signed up and published their own menu. There are no sample or placeholder restaurants here, so if a kitchen appears it is a real kitchen you can order from.

How does a restaurant get listed?

Publish the restaurant from the Cabana partner hub, then add dishes with prices and photos from your phone. Mark a dish as new, as a favourite or as an offer and it moves to the front of this page. It is free and there is no commission.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. Restaurants on Cabana take M-Pesa, and many take cash or card at the counter as well.
