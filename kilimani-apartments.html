Why travellers base themselves here

Kilimani holds Nairobi's densest concentration of purpose-built short-stay apartments — glass towers with pools, gyms and backup power that went up fast over the last decade. Yaya Centre and Adlife Plaza cover groceries (Chandarana, Carrefour), pharmacies and banks on foot; Argwings Kodhek and Lenana Roads carry the restaurants and cafés. The neighbourhood borders Hurlingham and Kileleshwa and sits midway between the CBD and Westlands, which is exactly why NGO staff, consultants and returning diaspora keep choosing it.

Prices and picking a building

Value is Kilimani's edge. Studios typically run KES 2,500–4,500 a night and one-bedrooms KES 3,500–7,000; two-bedrooms for families or shared trips sit around KES 6,000–11,000. Newer towers off Ring Road Kilimani and around Rose Avenue skew higher but bring rooftop pools and serious gyms. Every Apatmento listing shows the host's own price — nothing added on top.

Getting around from Kilimani

Ride-hailing pickups are instant along the main roads, and the CBD is 10–20 minutes off-peak. For flights, drivers run Valley Road onto Mombasa Road and the Expressway — plan 25–45 minutes to JKIA depending on the hour. Evenings out, Westlands' nightlife is a short hop; closer to home, the Hurlingham and Lenana Road strips keep late kitchens.

Good to know

Is Kilimani a good area to stay in Nairobi?
It's the most popular short-stay base in the city: central, packed with modern apartments, walkable to Yaya Centre, and midway between the CBD and Westlands. It suits business travellers, NGO staff and first-time visitors alike.
How much is a short-stay apartment in Kilimani per night?
Typically KES 2,500–4,500 for a studio, KES 3,500–7,000 for a one-bedroom and KES 6,000–11,000 for a two-bedroom. On Apatmento the host's listed price is exactly what you pay.
Is Kilimani safe for solo travellers?
Kilimani is a busy, well-lit neighbourhood by Nairobi standards. Follow normal city practice: use ride-hailing at night rather than walking dark side streets, and keep valuables low-key.
How long is the ride from Kilimani to JKIA airport?
Usually 25–45 minutes via Valley Road and the Nairobi Expressway, depending on traffic. Allow extra at morning and evening peaks.
