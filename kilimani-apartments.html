Kilimani's position in the city

Kilimani is where Nairobi's short-stay infrastructure is most developed. The last decade produced a generation of purpose-built apartment towers with rooftop pools, fully equipped gyms and reliable backup power. Yaya Centre and Adlife Plaza are walkable for groceries, pharmacies and banking. The neighbourhood sits centrally between the CBD and Westlands, which is why it draws NGO professionals, management consultants, corporate travellers and returning diaspora in roughly equal measure.

Rates and what to expect

Kilimani offers the strongest value-to-standard ratio in Nairobi's short-stay market. Studios from KES 2,500–4,500, one-bedrooms at KES 3,500–7,000, two-bedrooms at KES 6,000–11,000. Newer developments around Rose Avenue and Ring Road Kilimani sit at the top of those ranges with premium amenities. Every listing price on Apatmento is what the host charges and what you pay.

Transport and access

Ride-hailing access on the main Kilimani roads is immediate. The CBD is 10–20 minutes off-peak; JKIA via Valley Road and the Expressway is 25–45 minutes. Westlands nightlife is a 10-minute drive. The Hurlingham and Lenana Road restaurant strips are within the neighbourhood.

Kilimani: frequently asked

Why do most visitors choose Kilimani?
It has the most available inventory, the best price-to-quality ratio in the city, and central positioning that works for most itineraries. Business travellers, NGO staff and first-time Nairobi visitors are the primary guests.
What do Kilimani apartments cost per night?
Studios: KES 2,500–4,500. One-bedrooms: KES 3,500–7,000. Two-bedrooms: KES 6,000–11,000. The Apatmento listing rate is the rate you pay at checkout.
Is Kilimani suitable for solo travel?
Kilimani is a busy, well-lit neighbourhood by Nairobi standards. Follow normal city practice: use ride-hailing at night rather than walking dark side streets, and keep valuables low-key.
How long is the ride from Kilimani to JKIA airport?
Usually 25–45 minutes via Valley Road and the Nairobi Expressway, depending on traffic. Allow extra at morning and evening peaks.
