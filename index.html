The Story
Apatmento Cabana

A new name.
The same promise, bigger.

Where it began

Apatmento started with one idea that felt radical in Kenya: what if hosts kept every shilling they earned? No commission, no hidden markup — just transparent, honest bookings. We launched with apartments in Nairobi, and guests discovered a new way to travel.

Why we needed to grow

You asked for more. Flights, safaris, food, rides, events — you wanted one place for your entire life, not just your accommodation. Apatmento, as a name, spoke to apartments. But what we were building was far larger: a full travel and lifestyle super-app. The name had to catch up.

Choosing Cabana

A cabana is a place of ease and welcome — where you arrive, drop your bags, and feel instantly at home. Whether that’s a beachside apartment in Diani, a morning safari in Amboseli, or a plate of nyama choma after a long day. Cabana is the feeling. That’s the brand we wanted to build.

What doesn’t change

Everything that made you trust Apatmento stays exactly the same. Zero commission. Hosts keep 100%. You pay only what you see. M-Pesa payments. The same team. The same accounts. The same mission. Cabana is Apatmento, grown into the name it always deserved.

Our commitment to you

A rebrand is a statement of ambition, not a signal that anything is wrong. We’re growing — into more cities, more services, more ways to make your life easier and your money go further. You’ve been with us from the beginning. Come with us into what’s next.
