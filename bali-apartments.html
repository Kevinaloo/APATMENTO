Bali: which area is right for you

Seminyak: Bali's most sophisticated resort area. Beach clubs (Potato Head, Ku De Ta), fine dining, boutique shopping. Sunset cocktails on the beach.
Canggu: the new Seminyak. Digital nomad capital of Asia. Excellent surf, cafés, coworking spaces, Batu Bolong beach.
Ubud: the cultural heart of Bali. Rice terrace walks (Tegalalang), monkey forest, traditional dance, yoga retreats, art galleries.
Uluwatu: Bali's best surf (Padang Padang, Bingin, Impossibles), clifftop temple, dramatic Indian Ocean views.
Sanur: calm, family-friendly east coast. Good for children, snorkelling, the fast boat to the Gili Islands.

Getting around Bali

Scooter hire (100,000–150,000 IDR/day) is the primary way locals move around. GoJek and Grab operate in most areas. Private drivers (250,000–400,000 IDR/half day) are excellent value for day trips. Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) is in south Bali, 30 minutes to Seminyak, 2 hours to Ubud.

Bali: frequently asked

Is Bali safe?
Bali is one of Southeast Asia's safest destinations for tourists. Standard precautions apply. Avoid Kuta's heavier nightlife scene if you want a calmer experience; Seminyak, Canggu and Ubud are very relaxed and safe.
What is the best area to stay in Bali?
Depends on your style: Canggu for digital nomads and surf; Seminyak for beach clubs and food; Ubud for culture, yoga and rice terraces; Uluwatu for world-class surf and dramatic scenery.
Best time to visit Bali?
April–October (dry season) is the best time. July–August is peak. November–March is the wet season. Cheaper but afternoons often rain. Shoulder seasons (April–May and September–October) offer the best balance.
