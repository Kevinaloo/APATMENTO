Victoria Island: the business choice

Victoria Island (VI) is the de facto centre of Lagos's commercial, diplomatic and lifestyle activity. It is a planned island connected to Lagos Island by bridges, housing every major Nigerian bank headquarters, international embassies, the Nigerian Stock Exchange, and the city's finest restaurants.

Best for: Business visitors, first-time Lagos visitors, anyone who wants the full Lagos experience without navigating the sprawl.

What's here: Bar Beach, Eko Atlantic City (Africa's most ambitious real estate project, reclaimed from the Atlantic), Nok by Alara, Craft Grill, all major banks, US Consulate and European missions.

Typical prices: ₦120,000–₦350,000/night (1 bedroom). Premium for Eko Atlantic views.

Lekki: the new Lagos

Lekki Phase 1 has transformed into the most vibrant residential and dining area in Lagos. Admiralty Way is lined with the best restaurants in Nigeria. Spice Route, Sage, Nok, Shiro, Fireplace and dozens more. The apartment towers on Admiralty Way and the surrounding streets offer some of Lagos's most modern short-stay stock at slightly better value than VI.

Best for: Longer stays, younger professionals, anyone who wants the best Lagos food scene at their doorstep.

Lekki Phase 2 is newer and quieter with more modern builds. Chevron/VGC offers gated estate security. Ajah is more affordable but requires more planning for transport.

Typical prices: ₦80,000–₦220,000/night (1 bedroom). Best value in the VI-Lekki belt.

Ikoyi: the exclusive choice

Ikoyi is Lagos's most prestigious residential island. Quieter and greener than VI, with larger plots, mature trees and the city's highest property values. Banana Island (an artificial island connected to Ikoyi) has the most exclusive addresses in Nigeria.

Best for: Senior executives, those prioritising security and quiet over nightlife proximity, longer stays at premium budget.

What's here: Parkview Estate, 1004 Housing Estate (iconic lagoon-view towers), quiet consular residences, excellent restaurants in Bourdillon Road area.

Typical prices: ₦150,000–₦500,000/night (1 bedroom). Banana Island significantly more.

Ikeja: the airport choice

Ikeja is Lagos's mainland capital and Murtala Muhammed International Airport's gateway area. The Ikeja GRA (Government Residential Area) is one of Lagos's most established and functional residential areas. Allen Avenue is Lagos mainland's liveliest commercial and nightlife strip.

Best for: Transit stays, airport proximity, government business at Alausa, budget-conscious mainland visitors.

Typical prices: ₦60,000–₦150,000/night (1 bedroom). Best value near the airport.

Lagos booking tips

  • Always factor in traffic. Lagos traffic is legendary. A meeting in VI from Lekki can take 15 minutes or 90 minutes depending on time of day. Plan your base around where you'll spend most time.
  • Book with confirmed generator. Power outages (NEPA) are common across Lagos. Confirm your apartment has a standby generator before booking. The best ones run 24/7.
  • Use Uber/Bolt exclusively. Never use roadside taxis in Lagos. Uber and Bolt are safe, metered and widely available across VI, Lekki and Ikoyi.
  • Water supply. Most Lagos apartments have borehole water and storage tanks. Confirm water reliability before booking for longer stays.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Lagos?
Victoria Island for first-time visitors and business. Lekki for the best restaurant scene and value. Ikoyi for luxury and quiet. Ikeja for airport proximity. All are well-served by Uber/Bolt.
How much does a short-stay apartment in Lagos cost?
A 1-bedroom apartment in Victoria Island or Lekki costs ₦120,000–₦350,000/night. Ikeja GRA is significantly more affordable at ₦80,000–₦150,000. All prices on Cabana are host-set with zero commission added.
Is Lagos safe for visitors?
Victoria Island, Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi and Ikeja GRA are safe for visitors with standard urban precautions. Always use Uber or Bolt rather than roadside taxis. Lagos is a major international business city with millions of visits annually.
What is Eko Atlantic Lagos?
Eko Atlantic is a new city being constructed on land reclaimed from the Atlantic Ocean, adjacent to Victoria Island. When complete it will house 250,000 residents and 150,000 workers. Africa's most ambitious urban development project.
