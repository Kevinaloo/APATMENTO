Driving in and around Port Louis

Port Louis is Mauritius' capital and the base for the island's lagoon and hiking circuits. A hired vehicle makes sense here when your itinerary leaves the centre — Grand Baie, Flic en Flac, Tamarin, Belle Mare and the routes out of the city are where ride-hailing gets expensive or unreliable.

Rates and what is included

Daily rates in Port Louis typically run US$32–312, with chauffeur-driven options at the upper end. Confirm mileage caps, insurance excess and fuel policy with the operator before you commit.

Cabana has no Port Louis car hire operators listed yet. Listing is free and commission is zero, so operators keep 100% of the day rate.

Self-drive or a driver

For Mauritius, a driver is often the better value once you price in local road knowledge, parking and the time cost of navigating unfamiliar traffic. Self-drive wins on longer, rural itineraries where you want the vehicle on your own schedule.
