Frequently asked questions

How do I order from a seller?

Pick a category or search above, then tap any item. That opens the Cabana support chat with the product and seller already filled in, so you can confirm stock, price and delivery.

What can I buy on Cabana Shopping?

Fashion and textiles, art and crafts, food and drink, electronics, books and gifts, sourced from Kenyan makers, artisan collectives and independent shops. The catalogue grows as sellers join.

How much commission do sellers pay?

None. Sellers keep 100% of what they charge. Cabana does not add a booking fee or a markup on the seller's price.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa and card are both supported.
