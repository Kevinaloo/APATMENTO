Frequently asked questions

How do I order groceries online?

Pick a store or category above, tap a product to open that supermarket's live catalogue, add to basket, enter your address and pay with M-Pesa or card.

Which supermarkets deliver near me?

Naivas, Quickmart, Carrefour, Cleanshelf, Zucchini Greengrocers, City Grocery and Jaza deliver through Cabana, with live prices in KES. Coverage is expanding.

Are the grocery prices accurate?

Yes. Every price is pulled live from the supermarket's own catalogue. Items sold by weight can vary slightly and some stores add a small packaging fee.

Can I pay for groceries with M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa and card are both supported at checkout.
