Frequently asked questions

How do I order groceries online?

Choose a store or product category on this page, tap the item you want, and you'll be taken to that supermarket's live catalogue. Add products to your basket, enter your address, and pay via M-Pesa or card. Your groceries are picked and delivered to your door.

Which supermarkets deliver near me?

Naivas, Quickmart, Carrefour, Cleanshelf, Zucchini Greengrocers, City Grocery and Jaza Supermarkets deliver through our platform, with live prices in KES. Coverage is expanding to more areas.

Are the grocery prices accurate?

Yes — every price shown is pulled live from the supermarket's catalogue in Kenyan Shillings. Note that items sold by weight may vary slightly, and a small packaging fee may apply at some stores.

Can I pay with M-Pesa for grocery delivery?

Yes, M-Pesa and card payments are both supported at checkout for a fast, secure and cashless grocery shopping experience.
