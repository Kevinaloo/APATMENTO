Getting started

From sign-up to
first commission in days.

1
Create your account
Sign up with Google or email. Takes under a minute. Your agent account is active immediately. No waiting.
2
Complete your agent profile
Add your name, contact details, and the best way for hosts to reach you (WhatsApp, call, email). A complete profile gets approved faster.
3
Request your first listing
Browse listings, find one that fits your clients, and send a partnership request to the host. Propose your rate and add a message explaining who you work with.
4
Verify your identity (30 days)
You have 30 full days to upload a national ID or passport. Everything works during this window. Verify and your account never restricts.
⏳

About the 30-day verification window

New agents have 30 days of full access before identity verification is required. We do this so you can start earning immediately without paperwork holding you up. Upload your ID within 30 days and your account is permanently verified. Miss the window and referrals pause, but nothing is deleted. Verify late and everything resumes from where it stopped.
