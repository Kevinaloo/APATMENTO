Cabana Match: The World's First Reverse Travel Marketplace

Every travel platform works the same way: you search, you scroll, you book. Cabana Match turns this completely on its head. Post what you need, and verified African hosts come to you.

The problem with traditional travel booking

Search. Scroll through 200 listings. Read the same description rewritten 200 ways. Compare prices that have arbitrary fees added at checkout. Book. Wait for confirmation. Repeat for the next service.

This is how Airbnb, Booking.com, Safari Bookings and every other travel platform has worked since the internet arrived. The guest does all the work. The platform holds all the power. The host waits to be found.

It works, but it is inefficient, exhausting, and fundamentally biased toward whoever has the most listings and the biggest advertising budget. It is not designed for the guest's actual needs. It is designed for platform revenue.

How Cabana Match works

  1. Post your travel needTell Cabana exactly what you are looking for. Dates, destination, type of accommodation or experience, group size, budget, any specific requirements. It takes 2 minutes.
  2. Verified providers receive your requestCabana's verified hosts, safari operators, event spaces and service providers in your destination area receive your post directly. Only verified, reviewed providers see it.
  3. Providers send you tailored offersWithin hours, you receive direct, personalised offers, each one tailored to your specific requirements, priced for your budget. No generic listings. Real human responses.
  4. Compare and choose the best offerReview all offers, ask questions directly, and choose the one that best fits your needs. Book through Cabana with M-Pesa or card. Escrow protection applies.

Why this is a genuine world first

Reverse marketplaces exist in other industries. UpWork for freelancers, Bark.com for local services. But no travel platform globally has built a reverse marketplace for accommodation and travel experiences at scale. Airbnb has not. Booking.com has not. Expedia has not. Safari Bookings has not.

Cabana Match is the first direct client-to-provider matching system in global travel. Built in Nairobi. Available to any traveller going anywhere on the Cabana network.

Cabana Match is covered by Cabana's zero-commission model. Providers receive 100% of what they agree with you. No platform cut is taken from the matched booking.

Who Cabana Match is for

For guests who know what they want

If you are planning a safari in the Masai Mara for 4 people in August, you know what you need. Rather than scrolling through 80 operator listings, post your exact requirement. Dates, group size, preferred conservancy, budget, and let operators compete for your booking.

For groups and special occasions

Weddings, anniversaries, corporate retreats, family reunions. When you need something specific and are willing to pay for it, Cabana Match finds the providers who can deliver exactly that.

For budget-conscious travellers

Post your budget openly. Hosts who want your booking at your price will respond. This creates genuine price competition among providers, in your favour.

For luxury travellers

Post your luxury requirement and your budget. The best providers in the Cabana network respond directly. No intermediary, no inflated pricing, no compromised transparency.

How to use Cabana Match

Cabana Match is available through the Cabana platform at cabana.africa. To post a travel need:

  • Create a free Cabana account
  • Go to Cabana Match in the navigation
  • Fill in your travel details (5 fields: destination, dates, type, group size, budget)
  • Post. Your request goes live to the verified provider network immediately
  • Receive offers within hours; book the best one through the platform

Payment is via M-Pesa or card through Cabana's escrow system. Your money is protected until you check in and confirm arrival.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cabana Match?
Cabana Match is the world's first reverse travel marketplace. Built by Cabana, Africa's zero-commission travel platform. Instead of searching for listings, guests post their travel requirement (dates, destination, type, budget) and verified providers come to them with tailored offers. No other travel platform globally offers this.
Is Cabana Match free to use?
Yes. Posting a travel need on Cabana Match is free. Booking through a matched offer is also free of any Cabana commission. Providers keep 100% of what is agreed.
How long does it take to get offers through Cabana Match?
Most travel needs posted on Cabana Match receive offers within 2–24 hours, depending on the destination and type of request. High-demand destinations (Nairobi, Lagos, Masai Mara) typically respond faster.
Is Cabana Match only available in Africa?
Cabana Match is currently focused on Africa. The continent Cabana was built to serve. As Cabana expands globally, Cabana Match will expand with it. It is the world's first reverse travel marketplace, period.
