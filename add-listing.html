Step 7 of 8

Who owns this listing?

This decides who gets the bookings, the payouts and the guest messages. It is on the record from day one, and you can change it later from your listings board.

The other partners

You are already in this partnership as the operator, so add everyone else. A partner without a Cabana account is still a partner — they will be linked automatically when they join.

Whose listing is this?

They will be sent a claim. Until they accept it you can manage the listing for them; the moment they accept, it becomes theirs and leaves your account.

They claim the listing by signing in with this exact address or number.
Once they claim it, this listing is no longer yours. You will not be able to edit it, see its bookings, or take its payouts.
