Why look beyond Airbnb in Africa?

Airbnb charges African hosts up to 20% host service fee on every booking, on top of the 6–12% guest service fee. On a KES 10,000/night Nairobi apartment, the host receives KES 8,000 and the guest pays KES 11,200. That's KES 3,200 going to a San Francisco corporation, not to Kenya.

Booking.com charges African properties 15–25% commission. A Lagos apartment listed at ₦150,000/night sees the host receive ₦112,500–₦127,500. Over a busy month, that's millions of naira leaving Nigeria.

Beyond commission, Airbnb and Booking.com were not built for Africa. Neither platform supports M-Pesa natively. Neither was designed with African payment rails, African guest expectations, or African host needs at the centre.

2–7. Other platforms reviewed

2. SafariBookings.com

The best-known safari-specific marketplace. Lists operators across East and Southern Africa. Commission varies (typically 18–22%). No accommodation. Good for pure safari packages but limited compared to Cabana's full ecosystem. Commission: High. M-Pesa: No.

3. Jumia Travel (now Travelstart Africa)

The most recognisable African online travel agent. Focuses heavily on flights and hotels rather than apartments or short-lets. Commission-based. Less relevant for guests seeking authentic local accommodation. Commission: Yes. M-Pesa: Partial.

4. Expedia (Hotels.com)

Good hotel inventory in South Africa, Egypt and Morocco. Very limited apartment/villa stock in East or West Africa. Commission 15–25%. No M-Pesa. Not designed for Africa. Commission: High. M-Pesa: No.

5. Hostelworld

Excellent for budget backpackers. Good hostel coverage in Cape Town, Nairobi, Accra and Kampala. Limited to hostels and budget guesthouses. Commission: 10–15%. M-Pesa: No.

6. Direct booking via WhatsApp

Many African hosts manage bookings directly via WhatsApp. Zero platform commission but zero escrow protection, no booking guarantee, and no standardised quality. Payment security is entirely on trust. Practical for returning guests; risky for first-time bookings.

7. Vrbo (Vacation Rentals by Owner)

Good inventory in South Africa and Egypt. Almost no presence in East or West Africa. High commission (5–13% guest fee plus owner subscription). No M-Pesa. Commission: Medium-high. M-Pesa: No.
