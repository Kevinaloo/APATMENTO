Africa's zero-commission travel super-app

Cabana is the only global booking platform that charges hosts nothing. Built in Nairobi, covering 50+ countries, powered by Africa's first reverse travel marketplace.

The one-paragraph pitch

Cabana is Africa's first zero-commission travel super-app, founded in Nairobi, Kenya. Unlike Airbnb (which takes up to 20% from hosts) and Booking.com (15–25%), Cabana charges hosts absolutely nothing, every shilling, naira and cedi stays with the African host. The platform covers stays, safaris, flights, events, rides, food delivery, car hire and roommate matching across 50+ countries, with M-Pesa payment built natively from day one. Cabana also pioneered Cabana Match: the world's first reverse travel marketplace, where guests post their travel needs and verified providers compete for their booking. Cabana pledges 10% of net revenue to African community development.

Why this is newsworthy

  • A genuine world first: No global travel platform, not Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia or any other. Charges hosts zero commission. Cabana is the first.
  • Cabana Match is unprecedented: The reverse travel marketplace concept does not exist in global travel. Post your need, providers come to you. A genuinely new interaction model.
  • Built for Africa, not retrofitted to Africa: Airbnb and Booking.com were built in San Francisco and New York. Cabana was built in Nairobi with M-Pesa native from day one, for the 90%+ of Kenyans who use M-Pesa.
  • The commission story is compelling: Booking.com took 15–25% from an Accra hotel owner for years. Cabana takes nothing. The numbers, and what they mean for African host income. Tell themselves.
  • Scale ambition: 50+ countries already. Every continent. A zero-commission model that, if it scales, would represent a fundamental restructuring of how travel revenue flows. Keeping more wealth in destination communities globally.
  • African tech building global infrastructure: Cabana is not an Africa-for-Africa story. It is an Africa-for-the-world story. A platform built in Nairobi that covers London, Tokyo, New York and Rio de Janeiro at the same zero commission.

The commission comparison: publishable data

These are publicly available commission rates from each platform's published terms:

  • Airbnb: Host fee 3% (split model) + guest service fee 6–15%. Or host-only model up to 20%. Total platform take: 9–18% depending on model.
  • Booking.com: Commission 15–25% from property. No separate guest fee typically.
  • Expedia / Hotels.com: Commission 15–25% from property.
  • Vrbo: 5% host service fee + 6–12% guest service fee, or annual subscription ~USD 500.
  • Cabana: 0%. Zero. No hidden fees. Permanently.

On a hypothetical Nairobi apartment listed at KES 10,000/night, 20 booked nights per month:

  • Airbnb host-only model: Host receives KES 96,000–100,000/month
  • Booking.com: Host receives KES 120,000–136,000/month (pre-VAT)
  • Cabana: Host receives KES 200,000/month. Always.

Cabana Match: explaining the world first

Every travel platform since the internet began has worked the same way: the guest searches, scrolls, compares, and books. The host waits to be found.

Cabana Match reverses this entirely. A guest posts their travel requirement. Destination, dates, type of accommodation or experience, group size, budget, and verified Cabana hosts receive that post and respond with tailored offers. The guest reviews competing offers and chooses the best. Booking happens through Cabana's escrow system at zero commission.

This is how complex transactions work in professional services (freelance platforms, legal services, financial advice). It has never been applied to travel and hospitality at scale. Cabana is the first.
