Booking flights from Nairobi, with a person on the other end

Most flight search returns four hundred rows and leaves the hard part to you. Which fare actually allows a bag. Whether a fifty-minute connection at a given hub is realistic. What happens to the rest of the itinerary if the first leg slips. The Cabana flight desk answers those questions before quoting, because a person works the route rather than a filter.

  • One all-in price per option, taxes included
  • No booking fee and nothing added at checkout
  • Domestic, regional and long-haul on the same request
  • A named contact if anything changes before you fly

Routes out of Jomo Kenyatta

Nairobi is East Africa's busiest hub, which is a mixed blessing: plenty of capacity, and plenty of ways to book the wrong thing. Long-haul demand runs to Dubai, London, Istanbul, Amsterdam, Mumbai and Guangzhou. Regionally the desk works Zanzibar, Kigali, Entebbe, Dar es Salaam, Addis Ababa and Johannesburg constantly, along with the domestic coast hops to Mombasa, Diani, Malindi and Lamu that often price better as part of a through fare than booked separately.

When the desk is worth using

A straightforward return on a route you know well is quick to book yourself, and the search below will do it. The desk earns its place when the trip has a wrinkle: a group moving together, an infant on a lap, a visa run with a fixed return, a connection you cannot afford to miss, checked baggage that costs more than the fare on a low-cost carrier, or dates you can move by a few days if it saves real money.

Common questions

How does the flight desk work?

You describe the trip. A person prices it across the carriers that fly the route and sends back a short list, each option with one all-in price. You pick one and we issue the ticket.

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within about 45 minutes while the desk is staffed. You get a reference and a live countdown the moment you submit, and a message as soon as your options are ready.

Do I need an account?

No. A name plus an email or phone number is enough. Your reference opens the request from any device.

Is there a booking fee?

No separate fee, and nothing is added at checkout. Each option is quoted as one all-in price for your whole party.

What if nothing good comes up?

We say so and you pay nothing. Asking costs nothing and commits you to nothing.
