Popular routes from Nairobi

Jomo Kenyatta International is East Africa's busiest hub. Top long-haul routes run to Dubai, London, Istanbul, Amsterdam and Guangzhou. Regional favourites are Zanzibar, Kigali, Entebbe, Dar es Salaam and Johannesburg, with domestic hops to Mombasa and the coast several times a day.
