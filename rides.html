STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you request.

Does Cabana estimate ride prices?+

No. The page does not calculate a fare from distance, time, traffic or demand. It displays only exact price cards published by an authorised admin, or an operator quote confirmed after your request.

Can I request a ride outside Kenya?+

Yes. All 54 African countries are selectable. Kenya is the first operational focus, while other markets move through operator matching, onboarding and live activation.

Can a request include boats, bicycles or horses?+

Yes. The mode catalogue includes road vehicles, micromobility, water transport, animal travel, accessible options and specialist air movement. Admin can activate, pause or add modes.

Does sending a request commit me to a booking?+

No. A request asks Cabana and relevant operators to confirm availability and price. You choose whether to proceed after those details are clear.
