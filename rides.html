Questions riders ask

How does naming my own fare work?

Cabana suggests a fair range from local rates for your route and the hour you leave. You set your fare. Nearby drivers accept it or counter, and you choose who takes you. Nothing is agreed until you accept.

How do I pay?

Directly to your driver, in cash, by M-Pesa or by card where the driver takes it. Cabana never holds your fare and never adds a fee on top.

What if no driver answers?

After two minutes the Cabana desk steps in and finds a partner driver for you. You can also raise your fare or schedule the trip for later.

Can I book a driver for the day?

Yes. Choose Hourly, set the hours and your first pickup. The car stays with you for meetings, errands or a day out.
