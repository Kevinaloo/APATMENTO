Pricing your African property

Pricing short-term rental correctly is the most important operational decision after platform choice. Too high: no bookings. Too low: you leave money on the table and attract guests who don't value the property.

How to price:

  • Research comparable properties in your suburb on Cabana and Airbnb. What are similar apartments charging per night?
  • Set your base rate 5–10% below Airbnb equivalent listings. You can afford to because you're on zero commission. The same income for you = lower price for the guest = more bookings.
  • Use dynamic pricing for peak periods: Christmas/New Year, school holidays, local events (Nairobi marathon, AfricaCom, major concerts). Increase rates by 20–50% during these periods.
  • Offer weekly and monthly discounts (10–20%) to attract longer bookings that reduce management effort.

Photography that gets bookings

80% of booking decisions are made based on photos. This is not an exaggeration. A well-photographed average apartment outperforms a poorly photographed excellent apartment.

  • Shoot in daylight only. Open all curtains and blinds. Turn off all artificial lights (they create colour casts). Use natural light.
  • Clean and de-clutter before shooting. Remove personal items, clear surfaces, make beds with hospital corners. Presentation matters.
  • Show every room. Bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living area, balcony/garden if applicable. Guests need to see the whole property.
  • Wide-angle shots first. Show the whole room from the door. Then add detail shots (bathroom tiles, kitchen appliances, bedroom view).
  • Use a real camera if possible. A modern smartphone in good light is acceptable, but if you know anyone with a DSLR or mirrorless camera, ask them. The difference in booking conversion is measurable.
  • Don't use ultra-wide distortion. Making rooms look bigger than they are creates disappointed guests and bad reviews. Accurate photos = good reviews.

Managing guests effectively

  • Respond quickly. Response time is the #1 factor in booking conversion. Reply to enquiries within 1 hour during business hours. Use Cabana's mobile notifications.
  • Provide detailed check-in instructions. Gate code, parking, Wi-Fi password, emergency contact. Send these 24 hours before arrival.
  • Generator information is essential. In Lagos, Accra and many Nairobi properties, specify your generator hours and coverage. Guests need to know.
  • Earn good reviews. Cleanliness, accurate description, responsive host. These three factors drive reviews. Reviews drive bookings. This is the core flywheel.
