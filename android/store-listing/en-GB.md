# Google Play store listing (en-GB)

Paste these into **Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store
listing**. They replace the text Google rejected on 7 Oct 2026 ("Cabana is
Africa's #1 zero-commission travel super-app").

## Rules for this listing (Google's Metadata policy)

Never write any of these about Cabana, anywhere in the listing, the
screenshots, the feature graphic or the icon:

- Ranking or performance: **#1, No. 1, best, top, leading, number one,
  largest, fastest, most popular, award-winning, App of the Year**
- Priority claims we cannot prove: **first, world-first, only, unmatched**
- Prices or promotions in the title or icon: **free, 0% off, sale, discount**
- Emojis, ALL CAPS words, or repeated punctuation (!!!) in the title

Say what Cabana **does** instead. Zero commission, hosts keep 100%, M-Pesa
built in and Cabana Match are all true and checkable. That is the brand.

`tests/store-listing.test.mjs` fails if a banned word gets back in.

## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
Cabana is a zero-commission travel super-app, built in Nairobi for travel across Africa and beyond. Book a stay, a safari, a flight, a ride or a table in one app, and pay with M-Pesa or card.

ZERO COMMISSION FOR HOSTS
Hosts, guides, drivers and restaurants keep 100% of the price they set. Cabana takes no cut from what they earn. Guests see one small facilitation fee before they pay, and nothing is hidden.

EVERYTHING FOR THE TRIP, IN ONE PLACE
• Stays: short-stay apartments, villas, cottages and hotels, from Nairobi and Diani to Lagos, Accra and Cape Town
• Tours and safaris: game drives, day trips and multi-day safaris with vetted local guides
• Flights: tell us the route and dates, and a flight desk comes back with all-in fares
• Rides and car hire: get around town, or hire a car for the road trip
• Events: concerts, festivals and nights out
• Food: order from local restaurants for delivery or collection
• Roommates: find a room or a flatmate in the city

CABANA MATCH: LET THE OFFERS COME TO YOU
Post what you need, where you are going, when, and your budget. Verified hosts and operators reply with tailored offers. Compare them side by side and book the one you like.

PAY THE WAY KENYA PAYS
M-Pesa is built in, with STK push straight to your phone. Cards work too. On stays, your money is protected until check-in.

TRAVEL WITH CONFIDENCE
• Hosts and operators are checked by the Cabana team
• Message your host or guide before you book
• Clear prices, with every fee shown before you pay
• Booking confirmations and reminders by notification

FOR HOSTS AND OPERATORS
List an apartment, a tour, a car, an event or a restaurant for free. Set your own price, manage your calendar and get paid by M-Pesa or bank. No commission, ever.

Cabana was formerly called Apatmento.

Questions? Visit cabana.africa/help or email connect@cabana.africa.
```
