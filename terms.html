4. Bookings and Payments

All payments on Apatmento are processed securely via M-Pesa through PayHero. When you initiate a booking:

  • An STK Push is sent to your M-Pesa-registered phone number to authorise payment
  • You authorise Apatmento to collect the full listing amount plus the applicable platform fee in a single transaction
  • The host receives the full listing amount directly. Apatmento retains only its fixed platform fee
  • Payments are processed immediately upon your authorisation; bookings are confirmed once payment is verified
  • You will receive an SMS and/or email confirmation with a booking reference number
  • Booking confirmation is subject to host acceptance where indicated on the listing

You are responsible for ensuring your M-Pesa account has sufficient funds and that the phone number on your Apatmento account matches your M-Pesa registered number.

6. Cancellations and Refunds

Each listing on Apatmento has a cancellation policy set by the host, which is prominently displayed on the listing page before you book. Common policies include:

  • Flexible: Full refund (minus platform fee) if cancelled at least 24 hours before check-in
  • Moderate: Full refund (minus platform fee) if cancelled at least 5 days before check-in
  • Strict: 50% refund (minus platform fee) if cancelled at least 7 days before check-in; no refund thereafter
  • Non-refundable: No refund on cancellation; listing price may reflect a discount for this policy

In all cases, the platform fee is non-refundable.

How to cancel: Log into your account, navigate to My Bookings, and select "Cancel Booking". Refunds (where applicable) are processed back to your M-Pesa number within 3–7 business days.

Host-initiated cancellations: If a host cancels a confirmed booking, you will receive a full refund of the listing amount plus the platform fee within 3–5 business days. Hosts who repeatedly cancel confirmed bookings may be suspended.

Force majeure: In exceptional circumstances (natural disasters, public health emergencies, civil unrest) Apatmento may, at its sole discretion, offer additional refund flexibility beyond the standard policy. We are not obligated to do so.

Disputes: Refund disputes must be submitted within 7 days of the check-in date to connect@cabana.africa with your booking reference. We aim to resolve disputes within 10 business days.

10. Referral & Rewards Programme

Apatmento operates a Referral Programme and a Points-based Rewards Programme. By participating, you agree to:

  • Referral commissions: You earn a commission on bookings made by users you refer, for a period of 12 months from the referred user's registration. Commission rates are: 20% of the platform fee for referred users, and 10% of the platform fee for referred hosts/providers.
