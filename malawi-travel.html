Why Malawi is worth the trip

Malawi is built around Lake Malawi — a freshwater sea with more fish species than any other lake on earth. The country runs on English, Chichewa, prices in MWK, and centres on Lilongwe with major hubs at Lilongwe, Blantyre, Mzuzu, Nkhata Bay, Cape Maclear. Flights route through LLW.

Where to stay and what it costs

Across Malawi, a night runs roughly US$20–140 at market rates, from simple self-catering apartments at the lower end to lodges and beachfront villas at the top. Those are guide figures for the country, not Cabana quotes.

When to go

The window most travellers want is May–Oct dry season. Rates and availability tighten inside it, so booking direct matters more — you are competing for the same rooms without a platform markup on top.

Paying for things on the ground

Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba. Cabana settles bookings through the rails people actually use — M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and cards — rather than forcing every transaction through a card processor priced for another continent.

Entry requirements

E-visa or visa on arrival. Check the current position with the Malawi immigration authority before you travel; entry rules change more often than guidebooks do.
