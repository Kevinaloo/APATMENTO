How M-Pesa payment works on Cabana

Cabana uses M-Pesa's STK Push (SIM Toolkit Push). The same technology used by every major Kenyan business from Safaricom to Kenya Power.

  1. Find and select your stayBrowse Cabana, choose your apartment, safari, event or service. Select your dates.
  2. Enter your M-Pesa number at checkoutEnter your Safaricom M-Pesa number (07XX XXX XXX or 2547XX XXX XXX format) in the payment section.
  3. Receive the STK PushYour phone receives an M-Pesa payment prompt automatically, no need to initiate. The prompt shows Cabana's Paybill, the exact amount, and a reference number.
  4. Enter your M-Pesa PINConfirm with your 4-digit M-Pesa PIN. The payment processes instantly.
  5. Receive confirmationM-Pesa sends you an SMS confirmation. Cabana sends a booking confirmation to your email and phone. The payment is held in escrow until you check in.

Important: Cabana's M-Pesa payment is fully encrypted and goes through Safaricom's official Daraja API. Your PIN is never shared with Cabana. You enter it directly on your Safaricom SIM.

M-Pesa safety and escrow protection

Booking via WhatsApp and paying M-Pesa directly to a landlord's phone number is common in Kenya, but it carries significant risk. If the property doesn't exist, or the landlord disappears, your money is gone. M-Pesa itself doesn't offer buyer protection for accommodation fraud.

On Cabana, your M-Pesa payment goes into an escrow account: not directly to the host. The money is only released to the host after you check in and confirm your arrival with your unique Cabana check-in code. If something is wrong with the property, Cabana's team handles the dispute and you are protected.

This is the critical difference between paying directly via WhatsApp and paying through Cabana with M-Pesa. Same payment method. Completely different protection level.

M-Pesa travel booking tips

  • Check your M-Pesa balance before booking. STK Push will fail if your balance is insufficient. You can combine M-Pesa and card payment for larger amounts on Cabana.
  • Keep the M-Pesa confirmation SMS. It is your proof of payment. Save it until after you check out.
  • Use the correct phone number. Your M-Pesa-registered Safaricom number must match what you enter at checkout. The STK Push goes to that number only.
  • Large payments may require Lipa Na M-Pesa Business. For very large bookings (above KES 150,000), Cabana may use the Lipa Na M-Pesa Business payment flow rather than STK Push. The process is equally simple.

Yes, on Cabana, all Kenya accommodation, safari, flight and event bookings can be paid with M-Pesa. Cabana is the only major global booking platform with native M-Pesa integration. Airbnb and Booking.com do not support M-Pesa.
How do I pay with M-Pesa on Cabana?
Enter your Safaricom M-Pesa number at checkout on Cabana. You will receive an automatic STK Push (payment prompt) on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to confirm. Payment is instant. Your booking is confirmed immediately.
Is M-Pesa safe for travel booking?
On Cabana, yes. M-Pesa payments go into an escrow account, not directly to the host. Money is only released to the host after you check in. Direct WhatsApp M-Pesa payments to landlords carry fraud risk. Always use a platform like Cabana for escrow protection.

Cabana (native STK Push for all bookings), Safarilink (domestic flights), Kenya Airways (M-Pesa Global), and some local property managers via Paybill. Airbnb and Booking.com do not support M-Pesa.
