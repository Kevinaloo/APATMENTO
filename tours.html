Frequently asked questions

Are the walking tours in Nairobi really free?

Free walking tours run pay-what-you-want. There is no fixed price. You tip your guide what the experience was worth at the end. Some nature tours carry a small park entry fee.

How do I book a Masai Mara safari with zero commission?

Tap any safari above, choose your dates and pay with M-Pesa. Operators keep 100 percent of what they earn and you get instant confirmation.

What safaris and tours can I book on Cabana?

Masai Mara game drives, Amboseli safaris, Naivasha day trips, Diani Beach excursions, Nairobi city walking tours, Kibera community tours, Karura Forest nature walks and Ngong Hills hikes.

Can I pay for a Kenya safari with M-Pesa?

Yes. Every safari and tour on Cabana can be paid with M-Pesa, and the operator is paid directly with no commission deducted.

Is it safe to tour Kibera?

Yes. Community tours are led by guides who live in the area, are built to be respectful and educational, and support local projects directly.
