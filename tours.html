      Tours, safaris and day trips, booked direct with local guides

      Cabana Tours is where you find and book game drives, day trips, city walks and multi-day safaris with the guides and operators who run them. You see the real price, the real departure dates and the guide behind every tour, and you can message them before you pay.

      • No booking fee, and no commission on the tour price
      • Every guide and operator vetted by the Cabana team
      • Pay the deposit by M-Pesa, the balance on the day
      Browse every tour →

      What you can book

      Game drives in Nairobi National Park, the Maasai Mara, Amboseli and Tsavo. Day trips to Lake Naivasha, Hell’s Gate, Mount Longonot and the Ngong Hills. Food, art and history walks in Nairobi and Mombasa. Dhow trips and reef days on the coast at Diani, Watamu and Lamu. Longer climbs and expeditions on Mount Kenya and beyond. New guides and destinations are added as they are approved.

      Questions travellers ask

      How does booking work?

      Choose a tour and a date, say how many of you are going and pay the deposit by M-Pesa. Your place is confirmed straight away, and you pay the rest to your guide on the day. Some tours are paid in full up front; the tour page always shows which.

      Can I talk to the guide before I book?

      Yes. Every tour has a Message button. Ask about pick-up, what to bring or a private date, and the guide can send you a private price for your group right in the chat. Their phone number is shared once your booking is paid.

      Is there a booking fee?

      No. You pay the price the guide set, and Cabana takes no commission on the tour price.

      How are guides and operators checked?

      Every guide and operator is reviewed by the Cabana team before their tours go live, and every tour listing is checked before it appears on the site.

      Are the free walking tours really free?

      Free walks run on a pay-what-you-like basis: there is no fixed price, and you tip your guide at the end. Some walks include a small entry fee, which the tour page lists.
