Why Westlands works

Westlands is where Nairobi does business and then goes out. Corporate offices, Sarit Centre and Westgate Mall anchor the days; The Alchemist, the Woodvale Grove strip and a rotating cast of rooftops carry the nights. Apartment towers here trend newer and taller than most of the city, many with serviced options — the natural pick if your meetings, your gym and your dinner should all be a walk apart. The Gigiri diplomatic quarter and UN complex sit one ridge over, which keeps demand (and standards) high.

Prices and where in Westlands to be

Expect a premium over Kilimani for the location: one-bedrooms typically run KES 4,000–8,000 a night, studios KES 3,000–5,500, and two-bedrooms KES 7,000–13,000. Towers on the quieter streets off Waiyaki Way — around Brookside Drive and toward Riverside — buy you sleep; anything beside the nightlife strip buys you convenience instead. The airport advantage

Westlands has the cleanest airport run in Nairobi: straight onto the Nairobi Expressway and out to JKIA in roughly 30–40 minutes most hours. Early flights hurt less from here. In the other direction, Village Market and the Karura Forest gates are a short drive for weekend air.

Good to know

Is Westlands a good place to stay in Nairobi?
Yes — it's the city's business and nightlife hub, with malls, restaurants and offices in walking range and the fastest route to JKIA via the Expressway. Pick Westlands for work trips and nights out; pick Kilimani for quieter value.
How much do Westlands apartments cost per night?
As a guide: studios KES 3,000–5,500, one-bedrooms KES 4,000–8,000, two-bedrooms KES 7,000–13,000. On Apatmento the listed price is final — hosts keep 100%.
How far is Westlands from JKIA airport?
Roughly 30–40 minutes via the Nairobi Expressway in normal traffic — the most reliable airport run of any central Nairobi neighbourhood.
Is Westlands noisy at night?
Around the Woodvale Grove nightlife strip, yes — that's the point. For quiet, choose towers on the residential side streets off Waiyaki Way, toward Brookside Drive or Riverside.
