The Westlands position

Westlands is Nairobi's most international district. UN agencies, embassies and multinationals anchor the business side; Sarit Centre, Westgate and a dense strip of restaurants and rooftops handle everything else. The apartment stock trends newer here than anywhere else in the city, and serviced options are common. Gigiri is one ridge over, keeping the neighbourhood's standards consistently high and occupancy consistently strong.

Rates and location within Westlands

Westlands carries a modest premium over Kilimani: studios from KES 3,000–5,500, one-bedrooms KES 4,000–8,000, two-bedrooms KES 7,000–13,000. The positioning within the suburb matters: Brookside Drive and Riverside side streets are residential and quiet; the Woodvale Grove side is the opposite. Pick based on your schedule, not just your budget.

JKIA access

The Nairobi Expressway access ramp is in Westlands. JKIA is 30–40 minutes in normal traffic, which is the best ratio of any central Nairobi neighbourhood. Village Market and Karura Forest are a short drive north for off-duty time.

Westlands: frequently asked

Who is Westlands best suited for?
Business travellers, conference attendees, and those who want the full range of Nairobi's commercial and social offer within walking distance. For a quieter stay at lower rates, Kilimani is the comparison.
What are typical rates for a Westlands apartment?
As a guide: studios KES 3,000–5,500, one-bedrooms KES 4,000–8,000, two-bedrooms KES 7,000–13,000. On Apatmento the listed price is final. Hosts keep 100%.
How long is the drive to JKIA from Westlands?
Roughly 30–40 minutes via the Nairobi Expressway in normal traffic. The most reliable airport run of any central Nairobi neighbourhood.
How is noise in Westlands?
Around the Woodvale Grove nightlife strip, yes, that's the point. For quiet, choose towers on the residential side streets off Waiyaki Way, toward Brookside Drive or Riverside.
