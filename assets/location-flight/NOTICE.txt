Cabana listing location preview

World outlines: world-atlas 2.0.2 countries-110m.json, derived from Natural
Earth 1:110m vector data. Natural Earth data is public domain; outlines are
generalized for a globe view and are not parcel or address boundaries.
https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Projection: d3-geo 3.1.1 with d3-array 3.2.4.
Topology decoding: topojson-client 3.1.0.
All bundled software licenses are included alongside these files.

Leaflet 1.9.4 (the existing Cabana map engine) is also hosted locally to
avoid a script-CDN dependency during the flight. Its BSD-2-Clause license
is included as leaflet-LICENSE.txt.

Pinned distribution sources:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2.0.2/countries-110m.json
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/d3-array@3.2.4/dist/d3-array.min.js
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/d3-geo@3.1.1/dist/d3-geo.min.js
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/topojson-client@3.1.0/dist/topojson-client.min.js
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/leaflet@1.9.4/dist/leaflet.js
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/leaflet@1.9.4/dist/leaflet.css

The neighbourhood map uses Cabana's shared ApaMap basemap and displays its
OpenStreetMap/Esri attribution. Its circle is the same 500-metre approximate
area as the listing's existing map. The animation does not request an exact
address or a separate, more precise point.
