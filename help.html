Every answer here is the same one APA works from, and it changes the moment the policy does.

What does Cabana charge?
A fixed platform fee per booking, never a percentage. Stays and rooms: KES 300 under KES 5,000, KES 800 at KES 5,000 and above. Tours, events, car hire, rides, food, shopping and flights carry no platform fee at all. The host or operator keeps 100% of the listing price.
