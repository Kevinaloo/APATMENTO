What zero commission actually means

When a platform says "zero commission," it should mean exactly that. The price the guest pays is the price the host receives. No percentage cut, no booking fee, no "host service charge." The host sets a price. The guest pays that price. The host gets that price. Full stop.

This is what Cabana offers. It is not a promotional period. It is not "zero commission on your first 10 bookings." It is the permanent, unconditional model. Zero commission on every booking, forever.

This is in stark contrast to every other major platform:

  • Airbnb: 3% host-only fee (sounds small) but also a 14–16% guest service fee. On a ₦150,000/night Lagos apartment, the host receives ₦145,500 but the guest pays ₦174,000–₦174,000. Total platform take: ₦28,500.
  • Booking.com: 15–25% commission from the host. On the same ₦150,000/night property, the host receives ₦112,500–₦127,500. Platform takes ₦22,500–₦37,500.
  • Cabana: Host lists at ₦150,000. Guest pays ₦150,000. Host receives ₦150,000. Platform takes ₦0.

Why can Cabana charge nothing?

This lets the core booking service remain free for both hosts and guests.

Cabana's pledge is permanent: zero commission on bookings, forever. It is built into the brand's founding architecture, not a promotional tool.
