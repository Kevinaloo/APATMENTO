Know where you stand

Good relationships.
Clear terms.

A strong recommendation starts with an agreement everyone understands.

Who decides how much I earn?

You propose a rate or fixed commission when requesting a partnership. The host approves the final terms for the listing. Cabana records qualifying bookings and their agreed commission; the host settles with you directly.

Is joining the network free?

Yes. Creating an agent membership is free. A membership does not automatically approve you to represent a listing. Each host decides whether to work with you.

What is an attributed booking?

A booking connected to your personal listing link under the programme’s attribution rules. The click window is 30 days, and your membership and listing partnership must be eligible. Your dashboard shows the bookings credited to you.

Can I also work as a creator?

Yes. The creator experience uses the same agent membership with your audience details added. You can switch between agent tools and Creator studio from your workspace.

Does verification guarantee host approval?

No. Verification confirms your identity for the programme. Hosts still make their own partnership decisions. If a host declines, you can approach other listings.
