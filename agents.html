04 / Before the introduction

A few details.
Then you're off.

The essentials, without the small-print feeling.

What does it cost to join?

Agent membership is free. Complete identity verification and follow the status and deadlines in your workspace. Each host separately approves your listing partnership; verification does not guarantee approval.

Who sets and pays my commission?

You propose a percentage or fixed amount; the host approves the final terms. Cabana records qualifying bookings and agreed commission. The host settles with you directly.

How are bookings credited to me?

Your personal listing links connect eligible bookings to you within a 30-day click window. Your membership and listing partnership must be eligible. See credited bookings in your dashboard.

Can I be a creator as well?

Yes. Add your audience details to the same agent membership, then switch between agent tools and Creator studio in your workspace. Explore the creator experience ↗
