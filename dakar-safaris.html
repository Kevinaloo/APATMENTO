What you can reach from Dakar

Dakar is West Africa's Atlantic peninsula capital — music, surf and Gorée. From here the practical targets are Gorée Island (UNESCO), Lac Rose, Djoudj bird sanctuary, Saint-Louis colonial quarter. Operators based in Dakar run these as day trips and multi-day circuits depending on distance.

What a trip costs, and why the number differs here

Expect US$28–1020 per person depending on length, group size and whether park fees are bundled. On Cabana that figure is the operator's own price. Aggregators typically take 15–25% from the operator, and that margin is priced into the rate you are quoted elsewhere — the same guide, the same vehicle, a higher number.

Booking direct with the operator

Cabana has no Dakar tour operators listed yet. The model is direct booking with zero commission — an operator keeps 100% of what they charge — so if you run trips from Dakar, listing is free and takes a few minutes. The costs and seasons above are independent guidance either way.

When to go

Nov–May dry season. Wildlife density, road condition and price all move with that window, so it is worth building the trip around it where you can.
