Diani: layout and orientation

Diani Beach Road runs the length of the strip parallel to the ocean. Properties are either on the beach side of the road or a short walk behind it. Restaurants, dive operators and supermarkets sit along the road between stays. The southern stretch toward Galu Beach is where kite conditions are strongest. Colobus monkeys cross the canopy via rope bridges throughout. It is a destination you settle into rather than depart from each day.

Rates and seasonality

Seasonality drives Diani pricing more sharply than any other Kenyan destination. High season (December–March, July–October): one-bedrooms at KES 4,500–9,000, villas at KES 10,000–25,000+. Green season (April–June): the same inventory at materially lower rates, with fewer crowds and still-swimmable conditions. The listed price on Cabana is the host's rate and the amount paid at checkout.

Access

Fastest route: light aircraft from Nairobi Wilson Airport to Ukunda airstrip, approximately one hour, landing minutes from the beach. Overland: fly or take the SGR to Mombasa, then drive south via the Dongo Kundu bypass, which has removed the Likoni ferry wait entirely. On the strip, tuk-tuks and bodas handle all local movement.

Diani: frequently asked

What is the best way to reach Diani from Nairobi?
Light aircraft Nairobi Wilson to Ukunda: approximately one hour. Alternatively, fly or take the SGR to Mombasa and drive south via Dongo Kundu bypass. No ferry crossing required on either route.
When should I visit Diani?
December–March and July–October: dry, peak demand, highest rates. April–June: green season, lower occupancy, better rates, conditions still good for most activities.
What do Diani properties cost per night?
In high season: roughly KES 4,500–9,000 for a one-bedroom and KES 10,000–25,000+ for villas; green-season rates drop sharply. Cabana adds no fee on top of the host's price.
Do I need a car in Diani?
The strip is walkable for most needs. Tuk-tuks and bodas cover anything further along the road inexpensively.
