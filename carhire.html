Car hire — self-drive and chauffeur

Hire a car on your terms with Apatmento. Choose self-drive freedom to explore at your own pace, or sit back with a professional chauffeur who knows the roads. From compact city cars and fuel-efficient sedans to spacious SUVs and 4x4s built for safari and upcountry travel, there's a vehicle for every trip and budget.

Car hire is ideal for business travellers, families, and anyone wanting the flexibility to move around without relying on taxis. Pick up in the city or arrange delivery to your hotel or the airport. With zero commission, more of what you pay goes to the vehicle owner and less to middlemen.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to hire a car in Kenya?

Typically a valid driving licence (or international permit for visitors), a national ID or passport, and a booking deposit. Requirements vary by provider and are shown when you book.

Can I get a 4x4 for safari in Kenya?

Yes — 4x4 SUVs and Land Cruisers suited to safari and upcountry roads are available for self-drive or with a driver-guide.
