Nairobi car hire questions

Should I choose self-drive or a car with a driver?

Choose self-drive for flexibility when you are comfortable with local traffic and your route is permitted. Choose a driver when you want airport meet-and-greet, several city stops or less responsibility for navigation and parking. Compare the complete written quote for both.

Can I collect a hire car at JKIA airport?

Some operators offer JKIA collection or a meet-and-greet transfer. Check the exact terminal meeting point, flight-delay policy, operating hours and any airport delivery charge before confirming.

Do I need a 4x4 for a Kenya safari?

It depends on the park, access road, season and weather. Ask the operator to approve your exact itinerary in writing. High-clearance four-wheel drive is the safer choice where roads are rough, muddy or remote.

How do deposits, insurance and fuel work?

Policies vary by operator and vehicle. Your quote should state the refundable deposit, insurance excess, exclusions, mileage allowance and fuel rule. Photograph the vehicle and fuel gauge at collection and return.

Can I drive a Kenya hire car across a border?

Only when the operator gives written permission and supplies the required vehicle and insurance documents. Never assume a standard rental may enter Tanzania, Uganda or another country.
