Three things that go wrong when you hire a car in Kenya.

The wrong vehicle

A saloon cannot safely handle most park roads, and the Mara's black cotton soil turns to glue in the long rains. We grade every vehicle against the road and the season, and refuse to sell you a pairing that will strand you.

The price that moves

The advertised day rate is rarely what you pay. Insurance excess, airport handover, seasonal uplift and driver overtime all land at the counter. Here they land in the ledger instead, before you decide.

The deposit surprise

Operators hold KES 15,000 to 30,000 on collection, and many travellers arrive without the headroom. We show the deposit for every vehicle from the first screen, and the zero-excess tier removes it entirely.
