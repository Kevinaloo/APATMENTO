The Cabana Road Standard

Useful before beautiful.
Beautiful because it is useful.

Africa is not one driving environment. A city transfer, a highland pass, a desert crossing and a wet-season park road should never produce the same recommendation.

01

Exact points

Country first, then any geocoded city, airport, landmark or address—never a ten-city ceiling.

Africa driving desk

The questions that prevent expensive surprises.

Before payment, ask the operator to confirm the exact itinerary, insurance excess, restricted roads, border approval, mileage allowance, roadside support and every handover charge in writing.

Do I always need a 4×4 outside a major city?+

No. Many inter-city corridors are paved and suit an efficient two-wheel-drive car. A 4×4 becomes important when the final approach, season, sand, mud, gradient or park rules demand it. Cabana grades the full route, not the destination name alone.

Can I take a hire car across an African border?+

Only with written operator approval and the correct ownership, customs and insurance documents for every country. Border availability and opening hours must be checked close to travel.

Are the route figures live navigation?+

No. Cabana provides conservative planning estimates and clearly labels their source and confidence. Traffic, roadworks, weather, ferries and border queues can change the real journey.

Why show both US and UK mpg?+

They use different gallon sizes, so the figures are not interchangeable. Cabana labels both and also shows metric units to make vehicle comparisons unambiguous.
