Good to know

Does Cabana charge a booking fee?

No. The operator sets the rate and keeps it in full. You pay them directly and Cabana adds nothing on top.

What about the deposit?

The refundable deposit is the operator's and is shown in every quote. They take it at collection and return it when the car comes back as agreed.

Can I drive across a border?

Only in cars the operator has cleared for it, with their written permission and the right insurance. Cars that allow it say so, and the trip check flags every crossing.

No car where I need one?

Post a car request. Verified operators nearby, and the Cabana desk, answer with priced offers. Accept one and it becomes a confirmed hire with the same handover code.
