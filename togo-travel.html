Why Togo is worth the trip

Togo is built around Koutammakou's mud tower-houses and a walkable beachfront capital. The country runs on French, Ewe, prices in XOF, and centres on Lomé with major hubs at Lomé, Kpalimé, Sokodé. Flights route through LFW.

Where to stay and what it costs

Across Togo, a night runs roughly US$20–100 at market rates, from simple self-catering apartments at the lower end to lodges and beachfront villas at the top. Those are guide figures for the country, not Cabana quotes.

When to go

The window most travellers want is Nov–Mar dry season. Rates and availability tighten inside it, so booking direct matters more — you are competing for the same rooms without a platform markup on top.

Paying for things on the ground

Flooz and T-Money mobile money. Cabana settles bookings through the rails people actually use — M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and cards — rather than forcing every transaction through a card processor priced for another continent.

Entry requirements

Visa on arrival. Check the current position with the Togo immigration authority before you travel; entry rules change more often than guidebooks do.
