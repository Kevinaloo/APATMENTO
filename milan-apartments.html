Milan's best neighbourhoods

Navigli: the canal district. Milan's most vibrant aperitivo scene. Brilliant for evenings.
Brera: the art district. The Pinacoteca di Brera, boutiques, Milanese bohemia.
Porta Venezia: diverse, lively, great restaurants from everywhere, LGBTQ+ hub.
Isola: the creative north. Design studios, excellent food, the Bosco Verticale vertical forest building.
Duomo area: central Milan. The Duomo rooftop, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, maximum central access.

Getting around Milan

Metro (4 lines) is excellent and fast. MXP (Malpensa) is 50 minutes by Malpensa Express train. LIN (Linate) is 30 minutes by bus or taxi. The city is flat and bikeable via BikeMi scheme.

Milan: frequently asked

What is Milan famous for?
The Duomo di Milano (one of the world's largest Gothic cathedrals), The Last Supper (da Vinci, must book months in advance), the fashion district (Gucci, Prada, Versace), and Navigli's canal aperitivo culture.
What is Milan Fashion Week?
Milan hosts two Fashion Weeks per year. February/March (autumn/winter collections) and September/October (spring/summer). The city transforms. Book accommodation very early during these periods.
Is Milan expensive?
Milan is Italy's most expensive city but still competitive with Paris or London. Navigli and Porta Venezia offer good value for the quality.
