03 / BEFORE YOU JUMP IN

Real talk.

The useful details.
Without the small print energy.

Do I need a huge following?

Add your handle and audience details when you join; each host decides whether you’re a fit. Membership and verification don’t guarantee a partnership or earnings.

How do I get paid?

Agree a percentage or fixed commission with the host. Cabana records eligible bookings, and the host pays you directly under your agreement. Your tracking link has a 30-day attribution window, subject to an active partnership and programme rules.

Are stays included?

Complimentary stays and invitations aren’t automatic benefits. Agree any hosted trip, deliverables and costs with the host before making plans.

What happens after I join?

Add your social details, complete identity verification, and request your first host partnership. Once approved, use Creator studio and your personal listing links. Always disclose when you may earn commission.
