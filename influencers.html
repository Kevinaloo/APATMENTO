The details, beautifully clear

Good questions.
Honest answers.

You deserve to know what a collaboration means before you say yes.

Do I need a huge following?

You can register your social handle and audience size. Hosts decide which creators to partner with; a following does not guarantee approval or earnings. A relevant audience and a thoughtful introduction help a host understand your fit.

Are complimentary stays or VIP invitations included?

They are not automatic programme benefits. A host may agree a separate collaboration with you. Confirm any hosted stay, deliverables, travel costs, or event access directly before making plans.

How does the commission work?

You and the host agree a percentage or fixed amount for that listing. Cabana records eligible bookings and the agreed commission. The host settles with you directly under your agreement; there is no guaranteed income.

What does my tracking link do?

It connects an eligible booking to the listing partnership you hold. The attribution window is 30 days after a click, subject to an active partnership and programme rules. Your workspace shows the bookings that were attributed.

What should I tell my audience?

Use your own honest description and make the commission relationship clear. Current prices and availability are confirmed on the listing, so your audience can check the latest details before booking.
